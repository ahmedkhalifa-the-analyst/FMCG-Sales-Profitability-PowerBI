# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis of revenue, profitability, products, customers, channels, promotions, and operational performance, the following recommendations are proposed.

---

## 1. Protect Margin Through Discount Discipline

The decline in the effective discount rate from **7.38% to 4.45%** was accompanied by an improvement in Gross Margin.

**Recommendation:**

* Maintain tighter discount controls.
* Set discount thresholds by product, channel, and customer segment.
* Require commercial review for discounts that exceed predefined limits.
* Evaluate promotions based on incremental profit rather than sales growth alone.

**Expected Impact:**
Protect Gross Margin while reducing unnecessary discount leakage.

---

## 2. Review High-Revenue, Low-Margin SKUs

Several high-performing SKUs generate substantial revenue but operate with relatively weak margins.

Ariel Automatic Gel 2L, for example, combines high revenue and profit contribution with a relatively low **24.93% Gross Margin** and high discounting.

**Recommendation:**

* Review pricing and discount levels for high-revenue, low-margin SKUs.
* Identify whether margin pressure comes from pricing, product cost, or discounting.
* Prioritize margin improvement without significantly damaging sales volume.

**Expected Impact:**
Improve profitability without sacrificing key revenue-generating products.

---

## 3. Reassess Underperforming Promotions

Promotion effectiveness varies significantly across campaigns.

The **Back to School Flash Promo** operates at only **15.32% Gross Margin** with a **20% discount rate**.

**Recommendation:**

* Establish a minimum acceptable Gross Margin for promotions.
* Review or redesign promotions with excessive discounting and weak profitability.
* Continue scaling promotions that generate strong contribution while maintaining healthy margins.
* Measure promotional ROI using incremental revenue and incremental profit.

**Expected Impact:**
Increase promotional profitability and reduce margin erosion.

---

## 4. Optimize Channel Strategy

Traditional Trade and Modern Trade provide a strong combination of scale and profitability, while E-Commerce and Cash & Carry show lower margins and higher discount rates.

**Recommendation:**

* Prioritize Traditional Trade and Modern Trade for profitable growth.
* Investigate the causes of lower margins in E-Commerce and Cash & Carry.
* Review channel-specific pricing and discount policies.
* Avoid applying the same promotional strategy across all channels.

**Expected Impact:**
Improve channel profitability while maintaining revenue scale.

---

## 5. Develop Customer-Level Profitability Management

Some customers generate high revenue but comparatively weaker margins.

**Recommendation:**

* Segment customers using both Revenue and Gross Profit.
* Identify high-revenue/low-margin customers for commercial-term review.
* Protect strategic high-profit customers with targeted account management.
* Use customer profitability rather than revenue alone when allocating sales-team resources.

**Expected Impact:**
Improve customer portfolio profitability and focus sales resources on higher-value accounts.

---

## 6. Strengthen Regional Growth Strategy

Cairo is the strongest market by Revenue and Gross Profit, while Giza records the highest margin.

Beheira and Sharqia also generate meaningful revenue.

**Recommendation:**

* Protect Cairo's existing revenue base while identifying opportunities for profitable expansion.
* Investigate the drivers behind Giza's stronger margin performance.
* Evaluate Beheira and Sharqia for additional distribution coverage and sales opportunities.
* Compare product mix, customer mix, and channel performance across governorates before expanding.

**Expected Impact:**
Support targeted geographic expansion based on both revenue potential and profitability.

---

## 7. Improve Operational Order Management

Returned, Cancelled, and Spoiled orders represent a material operational issue requiring further investigation.

**Recommendation:**

* Track non-delivered orders as a dedicated operational KPI.
* Analyze Return, Cancelled, and Spoiled orders by customer, SKU, channel, and region.
* Identify the root causes of non-delivery.
* Quantify the revenue and profit impact of operational failures.
* Set operational targets for reducing non-delivered orders.

**Expected Impact:**
Reduce revenue leakage and improve order fulfillment efficiency.

---

## 8. Focus on Profitable Growth Rather Than Revenue Growth Alone

The 2024 decline in Net Revenue was primarily associated with lower volume and fewer orders, while improved pricing and lower discounting helped protect margins.

**Recommendation:**

* Monitor Revenue, Volume, Orders, Average Order Value, Discount Rate, and Gross Margin together.
* Separate growth driven by volume from growth driven by pricing.
* Prioritize revenue growth that generates acceptable incremental margin.
* Avoid using aggressive discounting as the primary mechanism for restoring sales volume.

**Expected Impact:**
Achieve sustainable growth without sacrificing profitability.

# Overall Recommendation

The business should **prioritize profitable growth rather than simply recovering lost revenue**.

The 2024 results demonstrate that stronger discount discipline and improved price realization can protect profitability even when sales decline. The next priority should therefore be to recover volume and orders while maintaining disciplined pricing and promotions.

The recommended strategy is to:

**Recover Volume → Protect Pricing → Control Discounts → Optimize Mix → Reduce Operational Leakage**

This approach can support revenue recovery while preserving the margin improvements achieved in 2024.
