# Business Questions & Key Insights

This section presents the key business questions addressed through the FMCG Sales Analytics Power BI dashboard, along with the main insights identified from the analysis.

---

## 1. How did we perform YoY?

**Insight**

Net Revenue declined by **11.69% YoY**, from **296.49M in 2023 to 261.82M in 2024**, following a **14.40% decline in Gross Sales**.

However, Gross Profit declined by only **3.07%**, while Gross Margin improved by **2.62 percentage points**, increasing from **26.73% to 29.35%**.

**Business Takeaway**

Despite the decline in revenue, improved margin performance helped protect profitability, indicating stronger pricing and discount discipline in 2024.

---

## 2. Which months drove growth or decline?

**Insight**

Net Revenue remained relatively stable during **January–April 2024**, at approximately **24M–24.5M per month**, while Gross Margin remained around **26.6%**.

From **June onward**, the effective discount rate declined alongside a significant improvement in Gross Margin to approximately **32%**. This stronger margin performance was maintained through December despite lower monthly Net Revenue of approximately **21M–22M**.

**Business Takeaway**

The second half of 2024 demonstrated stronger margin protection, although this was accompanied by lower monthly revenue levels.

---

## 3. Which categories drive sales and profit?

**Insight**

* **Home Care** is the largest contributor to Gross Profit at **43.8M**, but operates at the lowest Gross Margin of **24.93%**.
* **Personal Care** achieves the highest Gross Margin at **31.37%**.
* **Beverages** combines the highest sales volume at **5.07M units** with a strong **30.97% margin**.
* **Packaged Food** generates **32.0M Gross Profit** with a **29.83% margin**.

**Business Takeaway**

Home Care is the largest profit contributor but has significant margin improvement potential, while Personal Care and Beverages demonstrate stronger profitability efficiency.

---

## 4. Which SKUs need attention?

**Insight**

**Ariel Automatic Gel 2L** is the highest-revenue and highest-profit SKU, but operates at a relatively low **24.93% Gross Margin** and the highest discount rate of **6.39%**.

**Juhayna Full Cream Milk** also requires attention due to its lowest Gross Margin of **23.19%**.

**El-Arousa Black Tea** combines high revenue with below-average margin and above-average discounting.

**Business Takeaway**

High-performing SKUs should not be evaluated only by revenue. Pricing, discounting, and margin should be considered together when prioritizing SKU-level actions.

---

## 5. Which channels perform best?

**Insight**

**Traditional Trade** and **Modern Trade** are the strongest channels, contributing approximately **55% of Revenue** and **56% of Gross Profit**, while maintaining margins around **28.3%** and relatively disciplined discount rates of approximately **5.6%**.

**E-Commerce** and **Cash & Carry** operate at lower margins alongside higher discount rates of approximately **6.49%**.

**Business Takeaway**

Traditional Trade and Modern Trade provide a strong balance between scale and profitability, while E-Commerce and Cash & Carry require closer monitoring of discount and margin performance.

---

## 6. Which customers matter most?

**Insight**

**Hyper One (Branch #29)** was among the leading customers by Revenue, while **Kazyon Market (Branch #49)** was among the leading customers by Gross Profit.

**Gourmet Egypt** branches showed strong sales contribution but comparatively compressed margins.

**Business Takeaway**

High-revenue customers should also be evaluated based on profitability. Customers generating strong sales but weaker margins may require pricing or commercial-term reviews.

---

## 7. Where is performance strongest?

**Insight**

**Cairo** is the strongest market in terms of Revenue and Gross Profit, while **Giza** records the highest Gross Margin.

**Beheira** and **Sharqia** generate meaningful revenue and represent potential areas for further growth and sales coverage analysis.

**Business Takeaway**

Cairo remains the primary commercial market, while Giza demonstrates stronger margin efficiency. Beheira and Sharqia present potential expansion opportunities.

---

## 8. Are promotions effective?

**Insight**

Promotion effectiveness varies significantly across campaigns.

**Buy 10 Cases Get 1 Free** and **End of Month Volume Rebate** generate strong commercial contribution while maintaining relatively healthy margins.

In contrast, **Back to School Flash Promo** operates at only **15.32% Gross Margin** with a **20% discount rate**.

**Business Takeaway**

Promotional success should be evaluated based on both sales contribution and profitability. High-discount promotions that generate weak margins should be reviewed before being repeated.

---

## 9. Is discount hurting margin?

**Insight**

The effective discount rate declined from **7.38% in 2023 to 4.45% in 2024**, reducing total discount spend by **48.35%**, while Gross Margin improved by approximately **2.62 percentage points**.

**Business Takeaway**

The results indicate that stronger discount discipline coincided with improved margin performance and helped protect profitability despite lower revenue.

---

## 10. Are operational issues material?

**Insight**

Non-delivered orders represent a significant share of total order activity.

**Returned, Cancelled, and Spoiled** orders require further assessment to determine their financial impact on revenue and profitability.

**Business Takeaway**

Operational performance should be monitored alongside commercial KPIs because non-delivered orders can create revenue leakage and reduce overall business efficiency.

---

## 11. What drove the growth or decline?

**Insight**

The decline in Net Revenue was primarily associated with **lower sales volume and fewer orders**.

However, improved price realization and a lower discount rate helped protect Gross Margin and limited the decline in Gross Profit.

**Business Takeaway**

The 2024 decline was driven mainly by volume and order contraction, while stronger pricing and discount discipline partially offset the negative impact on profitability.

---

# Executive Summary

The analysis indicates that **2024 experienced a significant decline in sales and net revenue**, primarily driven by lower volume and fewer orders.

However, profitability was relatively well protected due to:

* Lower discounting
* Improved price realization
* Higher Gross Margin
* Better commercial discipline

The analysis also identifies several areas requiring management attention:

1. **Margin pressure on high-value SKUs**
2. **High-discount, low-margin promotions**
3. **Lower-margin sales channels**
4. **High-revenue customers with compressed margins**
5. **Non-delivered orders and their financial impact**

Overall, the business demonstrated **stronger profitability discipline despite weaker top-line performance**.
